# ds_footers

## Purpose
The information utility registry. It controls the structure, legal links, and social credentials displayed in the website bottom (footer), ensuring branch-specific compliance and contact information across different jurisdictions.

---

## Retrieve This Table When The User Asks About

**Website bottom and page footer**
Contact info, site links, or legal notices. Retrieving the full footer configuration (Zones) for a specific store branch.

**Compliance and legal links**
Finding critical compliance URLs (e.g., "Privacy Policy") and social media profile links. Auditing stores with specific social credentials (e.g., LinkedIn).

**Company bio and navigation**
Retrieving the "About Us" summary or the mini-map of popular product categories featured in the footer.

---
