# ds_blog_posts_categories

## Purpose
The content tagging junction. It enables contextual content marketing by linking technical articles to the product taxonomy, allowing the storefront to dynamically display relevant guides on specific category pages.

---

## Retrieve This Table When The User Asks About

**Blog groups and article types**
News classification or content tags. Finding all blog posts tagged to a specific product category (e.g., "Safety Equipment").

**Contextual marketing auditing**
Listing all product categories associated with a specific blog post to ensure correct cross-promotion on the storefront.

---

## Co-Retrieved Sibling Tables
- `ds_blog_posts` — the technical article or news post.
- `categories` — the product family being tagged.
