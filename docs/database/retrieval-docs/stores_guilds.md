# stores_guilds

## Purpose
The industry membership junction. It assigns individual stores or branches to specific guilds, establishing their membership in franchise networks or trade communities.

---

## Retrieve This Table When The User Asks About

**Store membership and industry associations**
Branch community links or franchise affiliations. Finding which guild a specific store belongs to.

**Cumulative membership**
Identifying branches that belong to multiple guilds simultaneously (e.g., both "Hardware" and "Emergency Systems"). Counting how many stores are active in each trade association.

---

## Co-Retrieved Sibling Tables
- `stores` — the business branch.
- `guilds` — the industry or franchise association.
