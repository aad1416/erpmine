# Google Production Readiness

**Status:** Draft  
**Purpose:** Prepare the product and Google configuration for public Gmail connections.

This is a living, blueprint-level spec. Implementation details will be added as decisions are made.

## Outcome

Users can understand who is requesting Gmail access, review the product's legal policies, and authorize the production application through Google.

## Scope

1. Publish the privacy policy.
2. Publish the terms of service.
3. Complete the application's public identity and required domain information.
4. Configure the production Gmail authorization application.
5. Complete Google's publishing and review process.

## Completion

- The required public product pages are available.
- Google accepts the production configuration.
- The Gmail authorization application is ready for real users.

## Open decisions

- Public page locations and ownership
- Final production domain
- Google review and release sequence
